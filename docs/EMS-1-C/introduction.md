# Product Introduction

## 1. Function Introduction

C&I Energy Manager (EMS-1-C) is specifically designed for industrial and commercial energy needs, covering diversified scenarios such as photovoltaic, energy storage, charging, and consumption. It not only fully supports energy storage features but also enables convenient access to various equipment units within the energy storage cabinet, efficient data aggregation, flexible protocol conversion, secure data storage, as well as centralized monitoring and intelligent maintenance. It delivers unprecedented convenience and efficiency to energy management.

## 2. Applicable Scenarios

Applicable to industrial and commercial PV, storage, charging, and consumption energy management scenarios.

- Equipment manufacturers and system integrators can pair this product with energy storage systems to achieve full access to all equipment units within the storage system and enable coordinated strategy execution.
- Operations and maintenance personnel can perform online monitoring and safety alerts through local or remote software platforms, and obtain real-time equipment operating status.
- Commissioning engineers can perform local or remote upgrades, conduct rapid configuration and debugging, and address operational pain points effectively.
- Industrial and commercial facility owners require systematic energy management tools to improve energy utilization efficiency, increase revenue, and achieve optimal economic energy consumption.


## 3. Product Features

- Multi-interface compatibility: RS485, CAN, Ethernet, DI/DO, AI, USB, etc.
- Multiple communication methods: Supports cloud connectivity via 4G, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and other communication means.
- Local management: Embedded web management interface for local login, viewing, and O&M.
- Multi-platform access: Supports encrypted data transmission to Solarman or third-party platforms.
- Energy strategies: Supports local/remote energy policy configuration.
- Wide protocol coverage: Compatible with various brands of inverters, BMS, and power meters.
- Security compliance: Complies with EN 18031 IoT security standards, with data encryption and user privacy protection capabilities.
- Upgradable hardware and software: Supports firmware OTA upgrades and data export.
