# Product Introduction

## 1. Function Introduction

C&I Energy Manager (EMS-1-P) is designed for industrial and commercial energy needs, including photovoltaic, storage, charging, and usage scenarios. It supports energy storage characteristics and supports daily control functions such as access to various equipment units in the energy storage cabinet, data aggregation, protocol conversion, data storage, centralized monitoring and maintenance. It also supports a variety of basic energy storage strategies such as peak shaving and valley filling, demand control, backup power management, backflow prevention, and new energy consumption.

## 2. Applicable Scenarios

Applicable to domestic and international industrial and commercial energy management scenarios of light, storage, charging and use:

- Industrial and commercial owners use EMS-1-P to statistically analyze energy and optimize management, thereby increasing revenue and achieving optimal economic energy use.
- Installation and debugging personnel are used to install, configure, and debug products, providing full and fast access capabilities.
- Supports local/remote monitoring and management, alarm processing, and real-time operation status of equipment and systems for operation and maintenance personnel.
- Third-party equipment vendors, integrators, etc. can connect and debug equipment using a visual UI interface that is intuitive and clear.


## 3. Product Features

- Multi-interface compatibility: RS485, CAN, Ethernet, DI/DO, AI/AV, USB, HDMI, etc.
- Multiple communication methods: Supports access to the cloud through multiple communication methods such as 4G, WiFi, Ethernet, etc.
- Localized management: Built-in web management interface, which allows local login for viewing and operation and maintenance.
- Multi-platform access: supports encrypted data transmission to SOLARMAN or third-party platforms.
- Energy policy: Supports local/remote energy policy configuration.
- Wide protocol coverage: Compatible with protocol access of various brands of inverters, meters, and BMS.
- Security compliance: Complies with the ETSI EN 303 645 international security standard and has data encryption and user privacy protection capabilities.
- Software and hardware upgradeable: supports firmware OTA upgrade and data export.
- Strong expandability: SD card slot is reserved to support expansion.
