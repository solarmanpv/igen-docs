# Safety Instructions

- All installation work must be carried out by well-trained professionals.
- Disconnect the power supply before installation and connection.
- Do not open the product enclosure or connected devices to ensure safety.
- Local laws and regulations must be complied with when installing this device.
- Damaged equipment must be taken out of service immediately. If within the warranty period, contact the manufacturer for repair or replacement; continued use or self-repair is not allowed.
- The device may only be used within the specified voltage range.
- Do not place the device in high-temperature environments or near open flames, and do not immerse it in any liquid.
- Do not place the device where children can easily reach it.
