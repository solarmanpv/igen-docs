# Storage Requirements

If the meter is not put into use immediately, the following requirements must be met for storage:

- Do not remove the outer packaging, and inspect it regularly (inspection every 3 months is recommended). If pest damage or packaging damage is found, replace the packaging promptly. If the outer packaging has been removed and the meter is not put into use immediately, put it back into its original packaging and seal it with tape.
- The temperature and humidity of the storage environment should be appropriate (storage temperature: -40℃~+90℃, relative humidity: ≤40%RH, no condensation). The ambient air must not contain corrosive or flammable gases.
- Store in a clean and dry place, and protect the meter from dust and moisture. Do not expose it to rain or ground water.
- If the storage period is two years or more, the meter must be inspected and tested by professionals before being put into use.
